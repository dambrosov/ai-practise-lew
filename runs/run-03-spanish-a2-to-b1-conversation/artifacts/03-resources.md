# Resources: Spanish A2 to B1 Conversation

## Summary
Free resources for all six modules (M1-M6), one primary per module and 2-3 alternatives each, 18 distinct URLs, all fetched on 2026-10-02. This revision answers the learner's feedback. Podcasts are now only two alternatives (M1, M3) and no longer a primary anywhere. Every module has at least one free speaking-practice resource, either shadowing with audio dialogues or a language exchange. Primary resources total 18 h against 34 h of module estimates, and the rest of the time goes to the speaking-practice alternatives and the 6 h review reserve.

## Content

### Research approach
- Queries (this revision): Spanish shadowing technique and free practice, free Spanish language exchange (Madrid, Tandem), Spanish dialogues with transcripts for shadowing. Earlier queries (past tenses, subjunctive, workplace phrases, repair phrases) are kept from attempt 2.
- Selection: free (0 EUR) first, speaking and shadowing over passive listening, dialogue material organised by tense or topic, Spain usage where available, A2-B1 fit. Quiz sections on pages are skipped.
- Mode: free-only. `mcp__fetch__fetch` was not available in this session, so every URL was verified with `WebFetch` (the fallback in the web-research skill).
- Speaking column: items marked "speaking" are the free speaking-practice resource of that module (shadowing = repeat audio aloud with the speaker; exchange = live practice with a partner).

- Hours are my estimates from page length and module goals, not published durations.
