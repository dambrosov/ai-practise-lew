# Requirements: Spanish A2 to B1 Conversation

## Summary
The learner wants to move from A2 to a solid B1 in spoken Spanish within 10 weeks (2026-10-02 to about 2026-12-11) to handle everyday and work small-talk with colleagues in Madrid. The budget is 4 h/week, so the total is 40 hours. Only free resources are allowed. The learner wants no quizzes, formal assessments or certification. Five success criteria are defined.

## Content

### Learner goal and target outcome
**Goal:** By about 2026-12-11, the learner can hold a spontaneous conversation of at least 5 minutes in Peninsular Spanish with a native or advanced speaker on everyday and workplace small-talk topics. Topics include weekend plans, food, commuting, family, work tasks and past events. The learner handles simple misunderstandings by asking for repetition or rephrasing.

**Context:** The learner will talk with colleagues in Madrid and wants a conversational B1 level. The emphasis is on speaking and listening.

### Preferences
- Learning style: a mix of listening, speaking practice and short grammar explanations.
- Formats: audio and video listening, speaking or conversation practice, short grammar explanations.
- Pace: a steady 4 h/week.
- Avoid: quizzes, formal assessments and certification-oriented material.
- Variety: Peninsular (Spain) usage preferred where variants differ.

### Scope
In scope:
- Spoken interaction and listening comprehension at B1 level.
- Short grammar explanations that support speaking, mainly past tenses, future and basic subjunctive needs for B1 conversation.
- Everyday and work small-talk vocabulary and functional phrases.
- Peninsular Spanish vocabulary and usage.
- A week-by-week plan within the hour budget.

Out of scope:
- Quizzes, tests, formal assessments and checkpoints.
- Certification preparation (such as DELE).
- Formal writing and reading skills beyond supporting use.
- Paid courses, tutors and apps.
- Latin American variants as a focus.
- Specialised technical or industry vocabulary.

### Success criteria
1. The learning path totals at most 40 hours across 10 weeks, with no week above 4 h.
2. Every resource in the path is free (0 EUR), with no paid tier required.
3. The weekly plan mixes listening, speaking practice and short grammar in each week.
4. The plan covers everyday and work small-talk topics, and its final week includes a conversation task of at least 5 minutes.
5. The path contains no quizzes, assessments or certification steps.
6. The learner can hold the final-week conversation in Peninsular Spanish at B1 level, judged by the learner's own review against the stated goal.
