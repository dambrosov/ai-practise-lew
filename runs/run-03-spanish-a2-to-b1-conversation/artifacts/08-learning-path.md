# Learning Path: Solid B1 conversational Spanish for small-talk with colleagues in Madrid

## Overview
This path takes you from A2 to a solid B1 in spoken Peninsular Spanish over 10 weeks (2026-10-02 to about 2026-12-11), at 4 hours per week, which is 40 hours in total. It has six modules (M1-M6) in prerequisite order. It starts with listening and shadowing for Madrid Spanish, then covers past-tense narration, everyday topics and plans, work small-talk, and opinions and polite requests. It ends with sustained conversation and repair strategies. Every module has at least one free speaking-practice resource, either shadowing (repeating audio dialogues aloud) or a language exchange. These take 16.0 h of the plan. All resources are free. There are no quizzes, formal assessments or certification steps. You track progress with recordings that you review yourself. The final week is a 5-minute unscripted conversation (capstone CP5).

## Goals and Success Criteria
**Goal:** By about 2026-12-11, you can hold a spontaneous conversation of at least 5 minutes in Peninsular Spanish with a native or advanced speaker. The conversation covers everyday and workplace small-talk topics such as weekend plans, food, commuting, family, work tasks and past events. You can handle simple misunderstandings by asking for repetition or rephrasing.

**Success criteria**
1. The learning path totals at most 40 hours across 10 weeks, with no week above 4 h.
2. Every resource in the path is free (0 EUR), with no paid tier required.
3. The weekly plan mixes listening, speaking practice and short grammar in each week.
4. The plan covers everyday and work small-talk topics, and its final week includes a conversation task of at least 5 minutes.
5. The path contains no quizzes, assessments or certification steps.
6. You can hold the final-week conversation in Peninsular Spanish at B1 level, judged by your own review against the stated goal.

**In scope:**
- Spoken interaction and listening at B1.
- Short grammar that supports speaking: past tenses, future, and basic subjunctive frames.
- Everyday and work small-talk vocabulary.
- Peninsular usage.

**Out of scope:**

- Formal writing, and reading beyond what supports speaking.

### Prioritized gaps
1. C2, narrating past events. This is the most frequent need in small-talk and the largest grammar jump from A2.
2. C7 and C6, sustained speaking with repair. The goal is conversation, and fluency comes from how much you speak.
3. C1, listening to natural Peninsular speech. Weak listening blocks all real conversation, and this work trains your ear for everything else.
4. C4, workplace phrases. Work is the stated context, and fixed phrases are quick to learn.
5. C5, opinions, advice and polite requests with basic subjunctive and conditional. These are needed for B1, but they can stay in common frames.
6. C3, everyday topics and plans. This is a partial gap, mostly consolidation and range.

The path skips present-tense basics, core vocabulary and simple questions, because you already know them.

### Quick self-check (not graded; to confirm where to start)
1. Say aloud: "Last weekend I went to the cinema and then had dinner with friends." Expected: "El fin de semana pasado fui al cine y luego cené con amigos." If this takes more than a few seconds, or you used the present tense, start M2 from the beginning.
2. What is the difference between "Cuando era niño vivía en Madrid" and "Ayer viví un momento difícil"? Expected: the imperfecto describes habitual or background past, and the indefinido marks a completed event. If this is new, you need all of M2.
3. How do you say "Can you repeat that more slowly, please?" Expected: "¿Puedes repetirlo más despacio, por favor?" If you know it, your M6 repair work can focus on rephrasing.
4. Which sentence is correct, "Quiero que vienes" or "Quiero que vengas"? Expected: "Quiero que vengas". If you are unsure, start M5 from the beginning.
5. In Madrid, what does "vale" mean and how do you address a group of friends? Expected: "vale" = OK; "vosotros" for informal plural. If you do not know, you need all of M1.

## Learning Path

### M1 — Listening and pronunciation for Madrid Spanish
- **Purpose:** start a listening and shadowing routine early. It trains your ear on natural Peninsular speed, accent and local features (vosotros, "vale", "tío"). The routine continues alongside the later modules.
- **Competencies:** C1. **Prerequisites:** none. **Difficulty:** easy.
- **Primary resource (speaking: shadowing):** [123TeachMe Spanish dialogues](https://www.123teachme.com/learn_spanish/spanish_dialogues_index). Pick dialogues recorded in Spain and shadow each one 3-5 times.
- **Alternatives:**
  - [How to get better at speaking Spanish through shadowing (Berges Institute, Madrid)](https://www.bergesinstitutespanish.com/blog/shadowing) (shadowing method)
  - [How to Speak Castilian Spanish Like a Local](https://theinterngroup.com/our-blog/sound-like-local-how-to-speak-castilian-spanish)
  - [Hoy Hablamos podcast](https://www.hoyhablamos.com/) (optional, not scheduled)
- **Exercises:** E1.1 Shadowing a Madrid dialogue; E1.2 Gist-and-fillers listening log.
- **Checkpoint:** none (not requested). Milestone: week 2, listening and shadowing routine in place.
- **Hours:** 4 (02). **Weeks:** 1-2.

### M2 — Narrating the past: perfecto, indefinido, imperfecto
- **Purpose:** tell weekend stories and answer "what did you do" with the right past tense. Study the perfecto and indefinido first (week 2), then the imperfecto (week 3), then consolidate (week 4).
- **Competencies:** C2, C1. **Prerequisites:** M1. **Difficulty:** hard.
- **Primary resource:** [Spanish Past Tenses 101: Preterite vs Imperfect (Tell Me In Spanish)](https://www.tellmeinspanish.com/grammar/preterite-vs-imperfect/). Skip the quizzes and downloadable sheets.
- **Speaking (shadowing):** [FluencyProf free Spanish audio, "The Past" dialogues](https://www.fluencyprof.com/free-spanish-audio.html).
- **Alternatives:** [Preterite vs. Imperfect (VerbMaster)](https://www.verbmaster.co/spanish-verbs-lessons/preterite-vs-imperfect).
- **Exercises:** E2.1 Perfecto and indefinido: my week; E2.2 Imperfecto and story retell. Capstone CP1.
- **Checkpoint:** none (not requested). Milestone: week 4, can narrate the past.
- **Hours:** 8 (02). **Weeks:** 2-4.

### M3 — Everyday topics and plans: routines, food, commute, family, future
- **Purpose:** go beyond short sentences on the core small-talk topics and on plans (ir a / futuro). Start with consolidation.
- **Competencies:** C3. **Prerequisites:** M1. **Difficulty:** medium.
- **Primary resource (speaking: shadowing):** [FluencyProf free Spanish audio, present and future dialogues](https://www.fluencyprof.com/free-spanish-audio.html).
- **Speaking (exchange):** [HelloTalk, Spanish language exchange](https://www.hellotalk.com/en/learn/spanish). Talk through routine, food, family and plans with real speakers.
- **Alternatives:**
  - [SpanishBOOM dialogues](https://spanishboom.com/dialogues-in-spanish/) (not scheduled)
  - [Coffee Break Spanish](https://coffeebreaklanguages.com/coffeebreakspanish/) (free podcast seasons; optional, not scheduled)
- **Exercises:** E3.1 Five-topic mini talks. Capstone CP2.
- **Checkpoint:** none (not requested). Milestone: week 6, can talk about everyday topics and plans.
- **Hours:** 6 (02). **Weeks:** 4-6.

### M4 — Work small-talk and workplace phrases
- **Purpose:** handle routine work chat, meetings and simple requests in the right register.
- **Competencies:** C4, C3. **Prerequisites:** M3. **Difficulty:** medium.
- **Primary resource:** [Spanish to Use at Work Meetings (SpanishDict)](https://www.spanishdict.com/guide/spanish-to-use-at-work-meetings).
- **Speaking (exchange):** [Language.Exchange, Spanish](https://en.language.exchange/language/ES-Spanish/). Ask a partner from Spain to role-play office small-talk and meeting phrases.
- **Alternatives:** [Be Polite in Spanish (SpanishDict)](https://www.spanishdict.com/guide/be-polite-in-spanish); cross-cutting [Spanish Filler Words to Sound More Natural (FluentU)](https://www.fluentu.com/blog/spanish/spanish-filler-words/).

- **Checkpoint:** none (not requested). Milestone: week 7, can handle routine work chat.
- **Hours:** 5 (02). **Weeks:** 6-7.

### M5 — Opinions, advice and polite requests (conditional, imperative, basic subjunctive)
- **Purpose:** give and justify your views, make suggestions, and soften requests using common frames (creo que, quiero que, me gustaría, podrías). The subjunctive stays limited to set frames.
- **Competencies:** C5. **Prerequisites:** M2. **Difficulty:** hard.
- **Primary resource:** [Spanish Present Subjunctive (SpanishDict)](https://www.spanishdict.com/guide/spanish-present-subjunctive). Use only the wishes, recommendations and doubt lessons, and skip the practice quizzes.
- **Speaking (shadowing):** [FluencyProf free Spanish audio, conditional and subjunctive dialogues](https://www.fluencyprof.com/free-spanish-audio.html).
- **Alternatives:** [Conditional Tense Spanish (SpanishDict)](https://www.spanishdict.com/guide/conditional-tense) (not scheduled; Be Polite in Spanish already covers the conditional for requests).
- **Exercises:** E5.1 Opinion, advice and request frames. Capstone CP4.
- **Checkpoint:** none (not requested). Milestone: week 9, can give opinions, advice and polite requests.
- **Hours:** 6 (02). **Weeks:** 7-9.

### M6 — Sustained conversation and repair strategies
- **Purpose:** keep talking for 5+ minutes without switching to English. Use repair phrases, circumlocution and turn-taking.
- **Competencies:** C6, C7. **Prerequisites:** M2, M3, M4, M5. **Difficulty:** medium.
- **Primary resource (speaking: exchange):** [HelloTalk, Spanish language exchange](https://www.hellotalk.com/en/learn/spanish). The free tier includes voice rooms.
- **Speaking (exchange):** [Language.Exchange, Spanish](https://en.language.exchange/language/ES-Spanish/). Use it for 5-minute unrehearsed calls with a partner from Spain.
- **Alternatives:**
  - [Asking "Can You Repeat That?" in Spanish (SpanishStep)](https://spanishstep.com/archives/5909)
  - [Spanish and International Friends in Madrid, language exchanges (Meetup)](https://www.meetup.com/language-exchange-madrid/) (in person, Madrid only; not scheduled)
- **Exercises:** E6.1 Repair-phrase conversation drill. Capstone CP5.
- **Checkpoint:** none (not requested). Milestone: week 10, capstone 5-minute unscripted conversation.
- **Hours:** 5 (02). **Weeks:** 9-10.

- You speak or record every exercise aloud.
