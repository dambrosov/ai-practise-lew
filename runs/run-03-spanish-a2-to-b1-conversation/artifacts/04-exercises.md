# Exercises and Capstone: Spanish A2 to B1 Conversation

## Summary
Seven speaking-and-listening exercises (E1.1 to E6.1) cover modules M1 to M6 and total 6.0 h, which is the 15 % practice reserve (6 h of the 40 h budget) set in 02. One capstone, "My Madrid Colleague Conversation", adds 3.0 h. Those 3.0 h sit inside the module hours of M2 to M6 (34 h in 02), so the 40 h cap holds. Practice totals 9.0 h. Nothing is a quiz or graded test. Each "done when" line is a self-review against a plain criterion, which fits the learner's no-assessment preference. The capstone ends in the 5-minute spontaneous conversation required by the goal.

## Content

### Practice principles
- Style: output-first. Each exercise is spoken or recorded aloud, with short listening input beforehand and at most 10 minutes of grammar reading.
- Cadence: one exercise block of 30 to 60 minutes per week, inside the 4 h weekly budget. Capstone milestones are short additions to the module weeks.
- Feedback loop: no tests. The learner records audio on a phone or PC, replays it against a written "done when" checklist, and re-records once. A free conversation partner (language exchange, 02 Open Issues) is used where available; every exercise has a solo fallback.
- Peninsular focus: use vosotros, "vale", "tío" and Madrid-based audio where the resource list (03) offers it.
- Resources are free only. Exact titles and links come from 03-resources.md; this file names only the type of material.

### Exercises by module

#### M1 — Listening and pronunciation for Madrid Spanish
| Field | E1.1 Shadowing a Madrid dialogue | E1.2 Gist-and-fillers listening log |
|---|---|---|
| Objective | C1 rhythm, reductions, accent | C1 gist of natural-speed speech |
| Steps | 1. Pick a 1 to 2 minute free Peninsular audio clip with transcript. 2. Listen twice without text. 3. Read the transcript, mark "vale", "tío", "vosotros" and fillers. 4. Shadow the clip 3 times, 5 sentences at a time. 5. Record the last attempt. | 1. Choose 3 clips of 2 to 3 minutes (podcast, vlog, interview). 2. Listen once with no pause and write 3 main points in Spanish or English. 3. Replay with captions and note 5 unknown phrases. 4. Write 1 sentence per clip using a new phrase. |
| Deliverable | One 1-minute recording plus a list of marked features | Log of 3 clips: main points and 5 phrases each |
| Est. hours | 0.5 | 0.5 |
| Difficulty | easy | easy |
| Self-check | You can say the clip at near natural speed and your recording has the same stress pattern as the original | You caught the main point of at least 2 of 3 clips before reading captions |

#### M2 — Narrating the past: perfecto, indefinido, imperfecto
| Field | E2.1 Perfecto and indefinido: my week | E2.2 Imperfecto and story retell |
|---|---|---|
| Objective | C2 correct tense choice for completed events | C2, C1 background description and contrast with indefinido |
| Steps | 1. Read a short grammar note on perfecto vs indefinido (about 10 min). 2. Write 8 bullet facts about your last weekend and today. 3. Say each aloud as a full sentence, using perfecto for "today/this week" and indefinido for "last weekend". 4. Record a 90-second account. 5. Replay and mark every verb form. | 1. Read a short note on imperfecto (about 10 min). 2. Listen to a 3-minute free story or anecdote in the past. 3. Retell it aloud in your own words, using at least 4 imperfecto and 4 indefinido verbs. 4. Record and replay once, then re-record. |
| Deliverable | 90-second recording plus verb list | Retell recording (about 2 minutes) plus a note of corrected verbs |
| Est. hours | 1.0 | 0.75 |
| Difficulty | hard | hard |
| Self-check | At least 80 % of your marked verbs fit the tense rule in the note | The retell includes both tenses and you can explain each choice in one sentence |

#### M3 — Everyday topics and plans: routines, food, commute, family, future
| Field | E3.1 Five-topic mini talks |
|---|---|
| Objective | C3 talking about a topic for several turns |
| Steps | 1. List 5 topics: routine, food, commute, family, weekend plans. 2. For each, prepare 4 key words only (no scripts). 3. Speak 45 seconds per topic, using "voy a" or the future for plans. 4. Record all five and replay. 5. Ask yourself 2 follow-up questions per topic and answer them aloud. |
| Deliverable | One 4-minute recording |
| Est. hours | 0.75 |
| Difficulty | medium |
| Self-check | Each topic reaches 45 seconds without switching to English; you used at least one future form |

#### M4 — Work small-talk and workplace phrases
| Field | E4.1 Role-play: a Monday at the office |
|---|---|
| Objective | C4, C3 routine work chat and simple requests |
| Steps | 1. Collect 12 phrases from free workplace-Spanish material: greetings, "¿qué tal el fin de semana?", asking for help, scheduling, polite requests. 2. Write 3 short scenes: coffee machine, meeting start, asking a colleague for a favour. 3. Play both roles aloud (or with a partner). 4. Record the 3 scenes. 5. Swap one phrase per scene for a different register (tú vs usted) and repeat. |
| Deliverable | 3-scene recording and the 12-phrase list |
| Est. hours | 0.75 |
| Difficulty | medium |
| Self-check | All 12 phrases are used at least once and each scene lasts about 1 minute |

#### M5 — Opinions, advice and polite requests
| Field | E5.1 Opinion, advice and request frames |
|---|---|
| Objective | C5 conditional, imperative and common subjunctive frames |
| Steps | 1. Read a short note on "quiero que + subjuntivo", "me gustaría", "podrías" (about 10 min). 2. Prepare 3 situations: a colleague asks for advice, you ask for a change, you give your opinion on a plan. 3. Say 3 sentences for each using the frames. 4. Record and replay; mark every subjunctive form. 5. Re-record the weakest situation. |
| Deliverable | 2-minute recording plus a table of 9 sentences |
| Est. hours | 0.75 |
| Difficulty | hard |
| Self-check | Subjunctive forms after "quiero que" and "espero que" are right in at least 6 of 9 sentences, and you can fix the others when you replay |

#### M6 — Sustained conversation and repair strategies
| Field | E6.1 Repair-phrase conversation drill |
|---|---|
| Objective | C6, C7 keep talking when words or comprehension fail |
| Steps | 1. List 10 repair phrases ("¿Cómo se dice…?", "Es como…", "¿Puedes repetirlo más despacio?", "Quiero decir que…"). 2. Choose a topic and speak for 3 minutes, deliberately avoiding 5 words you would normally use. 3. Use circumlocution instead of English. 4. Repeat with a conversation partner if possible, or with a recorded interview clip as the "other speaker". 5. Replay and count repairs. |
| Deliverable | 3-minute recording and a repair-phrase list |
| Est. hours | 1.0 |
| Difficulty | medium |
| Self-check | You used at least 5 repair phrases and did not switch to English once |

### Capstone project

**Title:** My Madrid Colleague Conversation

**Description:** The learner builds a small spoken portfolio and ends with an unscripted 5-minute conversation. It realises the goal: a spontaneous conversation of at least 5 minutes in Peninsular Spanish on everyday and work topics, with simple misunderstandings handled by repair. It reuses the recordings and phrase lists from E1.1 to E6.1.

**Milestones**
| Milestone | Module | Work | Deliverable | Hours |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CP1 | M2 | Pick the best past-tense story (E2.2) and re-tell it in 2 minutes without notes | "My weekend story" recording | 0.5 |
| CP2 | M3 | Combine routine and plans from E3.1 into a 2-minute "Mi semana en Madrid" talk | Recording | 0.5 |
| CP3 | M4 | Extend one E4.1 scene to 2 minutes with a work request and small-talk opener | Scene recording | 0.5 |
| CP4 | M5 | Add an opinion and a piece of advice to the scene using the E5.1 frames | Revised recording | 0.5 |
| CP5 | M6 | Hold a 5-minute unscripted conversation. Use a language-exchange partner or volunteer if one exists; otherwise answer a free recorded interview and talk in the pauses. Review it once against the criteria | 5-minute recording, plus one-paragraph self-review | 1.0 |

**Deliverables:** four short recordings (CP1 to CP4), the final 5-minute recording (CP5), a one-page phrase bank of the learner's own phrases, and a one-paragraph self-review.

**Acceptance criteria** (self-review, not a graded assessment)
1. CP5 lasts at least 5 minutes with no more than 2 switches to English.
2. It includes at least one past-tense story (perfecto/indefinido/imperfecto), one plan in the future, one work topic, and one opinion or request using a frame from M5.
3. At least 3 repair phrases are used naturally.
4. The learner marks 5 errors from the recording and corrects them aloud.
5. Peninsular features ("vale", vosotros if addressing a group) appear at least once.

**Est. hours:** 3.0 (inside the M2 to M6 module hours of 02).

## Assumptions
- The exercise hours are taken from the 6 h reserve in 02 and the capstone from the module hours, because 02 reserved no separate capstone time.
- The learner has a phone or PC with a voice recorder; no paid tool is needed.
- The conversation partner is optional; every task has a solo fallback.
- Self-review criteria are counts or yes/no checks, not scores, so they are not quizzes.
- Grammar notes and clips are chosen from free resources listed in 03-resources.md.

## Open Issues
- The final conversation (CP5) is only as realistic as the partner or clip available; the researcher should offer free exchange options.
- Self-review of grammar depends on the learner spotting their own errors; no external check is possible within the no-assessment constraint.
- The scheduler must treat the capstone hours as part of M2 to M6 time, not extra time.
