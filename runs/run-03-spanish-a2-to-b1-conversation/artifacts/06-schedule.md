# Schedule: Spanish A2 to B1 Conversation

## Summary
A 10-week plan from 2026-10-02 to about 2026-12-11 at 4 h per week, 40 h in total. Of that, 36 h are content (27 h study, 9 h practice including the 3 h capstone) and 4 h are buffer (0.5 h in each of weeks 1-8). This revision answers learner feedback in two ways. First, M1 and M2 are spread over weeks 1-4 instead of being packed into weeks 1-3, and M3 starts in week 4, so no early week carries more than two modules and each week is capped at 3.5 h of content plus 0.5 h buffer. Second, every module now has a scheduled free speaking-practice resource (shadowing or language exchange), 16 h in total. Module order follows the prerequisites from 02. There are no checkpoints, because the learner wants no assessments (05 was not selected).

### Adjustments
If the learner falls behind (use the weekly buffer first, then in this order):
1. Drop Language.Exchange in week 10 (0.5 h) and the Meetup/other alternatives, and keep HelloTalk and CP5.
2. Cut Spanish Filler Words (0.5 h) and the VerbMaster lesson (1.0 h, weeks 3-4); the Tell Me In Spanish article already covers the contrast.
3. In M5 shadow only 1.0 h of FluencyProf dialogues instead of 2.0 h, and take only the wishes and recommendations subjunctive lessons.
4. Shorten E1.2 to one clip, and replace the 1.5 h Language.Exchange block in week 7 by the 0.5 h shorter role-play (with a solo fallback, as 04 allows).
5. Do not cut E2.1, E2.2, E6.1 or CP5: past narration and sustained speaking are the top gaps (02).

If ahead:
1. Use unspent buffer for extra conversation on HelloTalk or Language.Exchange, aimed at past-tense stories.
2. Add Hoy Hablamos or Coffee Break Spanish episodes (03 alternatives) for listening, or the SpanishBOOM dialogues for more shadowing.
3. Do CP5 twice with different partners or interview clips and keep the better one.
