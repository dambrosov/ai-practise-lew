# Assessment: Spanish A2 to B1 Conversation

## Summary
The learner is at A2 in all skills and needs B1 spoken interaction for small-talk with colleagues in Madrid. The path has 6 modules (M1-M6) totalling 34 estimated hours against a 40 h budget, which leaves 6 h (15 %) for practice and review. The biggest gap is narrating past events fluently, because it needs the preterite/imperfect contrast. Close behind are sustained spontaneous speaking, and opinions and plans with basic subjunctive. Present-tense basics, core vocabulary and simple questions are already known and are skipped.

## Content

### Prioritized gaps
1. C2 narrating past events: most frequent small-talk need and the largest grammar jump from A2.
2. C7 and C6 sustained speaking with repair: the goal is conversation, and fluency comes from volume of speaking.
3. C1 listening to natural Peninsular speech: it blocks all real conversation and trains the ear for everything else.
4. C4 workplace phrases: the stated context, quick to learn through fixed phrases.
5. C5 opinions, advice and polite requests with basic subjunctive and conditional: needed for B1 but can stay in common frames.
6. C3 everyday topics and plans: partial gap, mostly consolidation and range.

### Prerequisite order
- M1 has no prerequisite; it starts listening practice early and runs alongside later modules.
- M2 requires M1 (basic listening routine; past-tense input is easier to notice by ear).
- M3 requires M1.
- M4 requires M3 (everyday phrases come before work phrases).
- M5 requires M2 (needs verb-form confidence and past tense for hypotheticals and advice).
- M6 requires M2, M3, M4 and M5 (integrates everything in sustained conversation).

Quick self-check (not graded; to confirm where to start):
1. Say aloud: "Last weekend I went to the cinema and then had dinner with friends." Expected: "El fin de semana pasado fui al cine y luego cené con amigos." If this takes more than a few seconds or you used the present tense, start M2 from the beginning.
2. What is the difference between "Cuando era niño vivía en Madrid" and "Ayer viví un momento difícil"? Expected: the imperfecto describes habitual or background past; the indefinido marks a completed event. If this is new, M2 is fully needed.
3. How do you say "Can you repeat that more slowly, please?" Expected: "¿Puedes repetirlo más despacio, por favor?" If you know it, M6 repair work can focus on rephrasing.
4. Which sentence is correct, "Quiero que vienes" or "Quiero que vengas"? Expected: "Quiero que vengas". If unsure, M5 starts from the beginning.
5. In Madrid, what does "vale" mean and how do you address a group of friends? Expected: "vale" = OK; "vosotros" for informal plural. If unknown, M1 is fully needed.
